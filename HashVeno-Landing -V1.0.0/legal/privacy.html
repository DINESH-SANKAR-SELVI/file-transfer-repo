01Introduction

HashVeno Technologies Private Limited ("HashVeno", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you entrust to us.

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal data when you:

  • Visit or interact with our websites, including hashveno.com and all subdomains;
  • Use our software products, including HashVeno Retail, HashRail, and HashRoute;
  • Engage us for professional services, including custom software development, cloud engineering, integration, or support;
  • Communicate with us by email, phone, contact form, or any other channel;
  • Apply for a job at HashVeno;
  • Interact with us at events, conferences, or through social media.

This Policy is issued in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and other applicable Indian data protection laws.

Plain-language summary

We collect only what we need to run our business and serve you. We never sell your data. We use industry-standard security. You have rights under Indian law, and we make it easy to exercise them.

02Definitions

Data Principal
The individual to whom the personal data relates, as defined under the DPDP Act. In most cases, you are the Data Principal.
Data Fiduciary
The entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data. HashVeno acts as a Data Fiduciary for the personal data described in this Policy.
Data Processor
An entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary. HashVeno acts as a Data Processor when handling customer data on behalf of our business clients.
Personal Data
Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
Processing
Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, sharing, disclosure, and deletion.
Consent
A clear, affirmative action by a Data Principal indicating agreement to the processing of their personal data for a specified purpose.
Sub-processor
A third-party vendor engaged by HashVeno to process personal data in connection with our services.

03Scope

This Policy applies to personal data we process in our capacity as a Data Fiduciary — that is, when we decide why and how personal data is processed, such as:

  • Website visitors and marketing contacts;
  • Prospective clients, existing clients, and their authorised users;
  • Vendors, contractors, and partners;
  • Job applicants and candidates;
  • Event attendees and social media interactions.

3.1 When we act as a Data Processor

When you use our Products, we may process customer data on your behalf. In such cases, we act as a Data Processor, and our processing is governed by the agreement between us and the applicable customer. Our obligations as a Processor are set out in the applicable Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

If you are an individual whose personal data is being processed by a HashVeno customer, please contact that customer directly to exercise your rights. We will assist them in responding to your request as required by law.

3.2 What this Policy does not cover

This Policy does not apply to:

  • Third-party websites, apps, or services that we do not control;
  • Data that has been fully anonymised and can no longer be linked to an individual;
  • Personal data processed by our customers on their own infrastructure.

  • Data from children under 18 (see Section 16).

05How we collect personal data

5.1 Data you provide directly

We collect personal data when you:

  • Fill out a contact form, request a demo, or book a call;
  • Create an account on one of our Products;
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or download a resource;

  • Respond to a survey, provide feedback, or enter a promotion;

When you visit our websites or use our Products, we automatically collect:

  • Pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs;
  • Error logs and diagnostic information.

5.3 Data from third parties

We may receive personal data about you from:

  • Business partners and referral sources — when someone refers you to us;
  • Public sources — such as LinkedIn or company websites, when researching prospective clients;
  • Service providers — such as analytics or CRM platforms you've interacted with.

06Why we process personal data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

6.1 To provide our Products and Services

  • Create and manage your account;
  • Deliver the features and functionality you've subscribed to;
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries;
  • Process payments and issue invoices;
  • Send transactional notifications (e.g., security alerts, service updates).

6.2 To communicate with you

  • Respond to your messages, questions, and requests;
  • Schedule and conduct meetings, demos, or calls;
  • Send newsletters and product updates (with your consent, where required).

6.3 To improve our Products and Services

  • Analyse usage patterns and feature adoption;
  • Identify and fix bugs, performance issues, and security vulnerabilities;
  • Develop new features and enhance existing ones;
  • Train internal AI and ML models using anonymised and aggregated data only.

6.4 To meet legal and regulatory obligations

  • Comply with tax, accounting, and audit requirements;
  • Respond to lawful requests from government or law enforcement;
  • Enforce our Terms & Conditions and other agreements;
  • Protect the rights, property, and safety of HashVeno, our users, and the public.

6.5 For recruitment

  • Evaluate your application and qualifications;
  • Communicate with you during the hiring process;
  • Maintain records for future opportunities (with your consent).

7.1 Withdrawing consent

Where we rely on consent, you have the right to withdraw it at any time by contacting us at privacy@hashveno.com. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

09How we share personal data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent, trade, or otherwise monetise your personal data.

We may share personal data with the following categories of recipients:

9.1 Service providers and sub-processors

We share personal data with third-party vendors who help us run our business. These vendors are contractually bound to protect your data, use it only for the purposes we specify, and comply with applicable data protection laws. See Section 10 for details.

9.2 Business clients (when we act as a Processor)

When we provide Products or Services to a business client, we may process personal data on their behalf. In these cases, the client is the Data Fiduciary, and disclosure is governed by our agreement with them.

9.3 Professional advisers

We may share data with our lawyers, accountants, auditors, and insurers where necessary for professional advice or compliance.

9.4 Legal and regulatory authorities

We may disclose personal data when required by law, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation, court order, or regulatory request;
  • Protect and defend our rights, property, or safety;
  • Protect the safety of our users or the public;
  • Detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues.

9.5 Corporate transactions

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

9.6 With your consent

We may share personal data with third parties when you have given us specific consent to do so.

11International data transfers

Our primary data storage is in India. Some of our sub-processors may store or process personal data outside India.

Where personal data is transferred outside India, we ensure it receives an adequate level of protection by:

  • Entering into contractual clauses that require equivalent protection;
  • Transferring only to countries that the Indian government has notified as permissible under the DPDP Act;
  • Using vendors that are certified under recognised frameworks such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2.

If you would like to know where specific categories of your data are stored or processed, please contact us.

12Data security

We take data security seriously and implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

12.1 Technical measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256);
  • Role-based access controls with least-privilege principles;
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access;
  • Regular vulnerability scanning and security testing;
  • Centralised logging and monitoring for suspicious activity.

12.2 Organisational measures

  • Security awareness training for all employees;
  • Confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements for all staff and contractors;
  • Documented incident response procedures;
  • Regular security reviews by external experts.

12.3 Data breach notification

If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in harm to you, we will:

  • Notify affected individuals without undue delay;
  • Notify the Data Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Act;
  • Take prompt action to contain, investigate, and remediate the breach;
  • Provide information about the breach and the steps you can take.
Important caveat

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are also responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

14Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

14.1 Right to access

You have the right to obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you, the purposes of processing, and the identities of the parties with whom we share it.

14.2 Right to correction and erasure

You have the right to request that we correct inaccurate or misleading personal data, complete incomplete data, or erase data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.

14.3 Right to grievance redressal

You have the right to have your grievance addressed by our Data Protection Officer. See Section 19 for details.

14.4 Right to nominate

You have the right to nominate another individual who can exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

14.5 Right to withdraw consent

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

14.6 How to exercise your rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@hashveno.com. We will respond within 30 days of receiving a verifiable request. If we need more time, we will inform you and explain why.

No fee

Exercising your rights is free of charge. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

15Marketing communications

We may send you marketing communications if you have subscribed to our newsletter, downloaded a resource, or expressed interest in our Products or Services.

Every marketing email we send includes a clear, one-click unsubscribe link. You can also contact us at privacy@hashveno.com to opt out.

Note that even if you unsubscribe from marketing emails, we may still send you transactional messages (such as security alerts, invoice notifications, or service updates) where necessary to provide the Products or Services.

16Children's privacy

Our Products and Services are designed for businesses and are not intended for children under 18 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you become aware that a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us so that we can delete it.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 18 without verifiable parental consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete such data as soon as possible.
