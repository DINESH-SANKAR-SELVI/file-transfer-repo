How it works

Five steps. No new workflow.

HashVeno V1 fits into how your shop already runs. The AI handles the tracking — your staff handles the decision.

STEP 01
👤

Customer enters

Face is recognised at entry when possible. If not, the system starts an anonymous session tracking them from the door.

STEP 02
📹

Camera watches the floor

Overhead cameras see products, shelves, and movement — using your existing CCTV setup, no shelf-level cameras needed.

STEP 03
🧠

AI identifies products & customer

Which item was picked. Which customer picked it. The bill builds quietly in the background as they shop.

STEP 04
✓

Staff confirms the bill

At checkout, the staff member sees the proposed bill and taps confirm. If the AI got it wrong, they fix it in one move.

STEP 05
🎯

AI learns from every correction

Every fix teaches the system. Over weeks, accuracy compounds — the AI learns your shelves, your customers, your shop's quirks.
