Why it matters

Ticket evasion and overcrowding cost more than money.

Every unchecked coach is a safety risk. Every crowded platform is a preventable incident. HashRail turns your existing infrastructure into a live safety net.

● The current reality

Manual checks can't cover every coach or platform

TTEs and RPF teams do extraordinary work — but they can't be everywhere. The gaps are where revenue loss and incidents happen.

✕ Unauthorized passengers detected only after the fact
✕ Crowd build-ups spotted visually, often too late
✕ No live data on coach-wise occupancy during boarding
✕ Post-incident reports built from memory, not records
● With HashRail

Every coach, every platform — monitored live

HashRail works with the cameras you already have. It gives your control room, TTEs, and RPF a shared, real-time picture of every train and platform under your zone.

✓ Coach-wise density measured continuously, not sampled
✓ Ticket anomalies flagged within seconds, routed to the right TTE
✓ Platform crowd alerts escalated to RPF and control room
✓ Every alert timestamped and archived for audit

FAQ

Questions, answered.

No. HashRail is designed to work with the CCTV infrastructure already deployed across Indian Railways. Our edge device connects to your existing NVR/DVR feed and processes the video locally, sending only metadata — not raw footage — to the cloud. This keeps bandwidth usage minimal and deployment fast.
Privacy is built into the architecture. HashRail detects and counts people and anomalies — it does not identify individuals. No facial recognition, no biometric storage. All processing happens at the edge, and no personally identifiable data leaves the station. Every deployment is aligned with Indian data protection regulations.
HashRail runs fully at the edge. The on-site device handles detection and alerts locally, sending only lightweight metadata upstream. If connectivity drops, alerts continue to work on the local network and sync automatically when the connection is restored. Nothing is lost.
HashRail ingests ticketed passenger manifests from your existing reservation systems. It then compares physical coach occupancy against the ticketed count in real time. The integration is read-only, non-intrusive, and can run through a secure API or a scheduled file exchange if preferred.
Pilot deployment at a single station takes 5–10 days from sign-off, including hardware installation and calibration. A full division rollout typically takes 6–8 weeks. Zone-level deployments are planned with a dedicated project team and phased across divisions.
