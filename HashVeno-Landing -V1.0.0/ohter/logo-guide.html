Recommendation

Go with A — Hashtag Node

It's the strongest of the three because it does the most work with the least ink. The # is literally in your brand name — owning it means competitors can't use it. One intersection becomes a network node, which carries the "Smart Network" tagline without needing a separate molecule illustration.

It passes every test that matters:

  • Favicon test — reads at 16px
  • Monochrome test — works in one color (invoices, stamps, embroidery)
  • Background test — works on dark and light
  • Scalability — no gradients, no gloss, no raster assets
  • Distinctiveness — no other Indian SaaS company owns the hashtag mark

If you want to keep the HV instead, concept C is your safest bet — same idea as your current logo, but flat and clean. It's less distinctive but more familiar to your existing customers.
