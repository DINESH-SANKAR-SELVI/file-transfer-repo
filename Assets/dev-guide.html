05 · Hosting Checklist

Before you put it live.

Everything to verify and configure before pointing your domain at the server.

📧
Email Addresses to Set Up
hello@hashveno.com — general inquiries
careers@hashveno.com — job applications
legal@hashveno.com — legal notices
privacy@hashveno.com — data requests
tutorials@hashveno.com — tutorial requests
support@hashveno.com — existing clients
🔗
Form Endpoints to Wire Up
Contact form — /contact page → email or CRM
Newsletter signup — appears on Articles + Landing → ESP
Careers applications — /careers → resumes inbox
Tutorial requests — /tutorials → content team inbox
🌐
DNS & Domain
A record for hashveno.com → server IP
CNAME for www → hashveno.com
SSL certificate (Let's Encrypt or Cloudflare)
MX records for email delivery
SPF, DKIM, DMARC records for email authentication
✓
Pre-Launch Verification
Test every nav link (top nav, mobile, footer)
Verify forms submit correctly end-to-end
Remove internal "review callouts" from legal pages
Replace placeholder testimonials with real ones
Update open roles on Careers page (or hide section)
Test on mobile (iOS Safari, Android Chrome)
Verify favicon appears on all pages
Legal pages reviewed by lawyer
