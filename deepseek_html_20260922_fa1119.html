Why work at HashVeno.

We're not the biggest team. We're not the loudest. We're just very deliberate about how we build.

🎯
Your work reaches production.

Small team means no layers between you and what ships. Engineers here push code that real businesses use the same week. No months of internal review, no gates that eat momentum.

🚀
Direct access to founders.

The people who started HashVeno are the ones you work with. No account managers, no middle layers. Feedback goes both ways — and decisions happen fast.

📐
Small projects. Big ownership.

Everyone owns something end-to-end. A feature, a product, a system. You'll never be one of thirty engineers on one ticket queue — you'll be the person whose name is on the thing.

💡
Learn by shipping.

We don't have formal training programs. What we have is a stream of real problems — POS systems, railway monitoring, fleet tracking — that force you to get good at things fast.

🌏
Real-world impact.

Our products run in Indian shops, on Indian trains, and across Indian bus depots. The work matters to people outside the office. That changes how you feel about Mondays.

🛠
Modern stack, real engineering.

React, Node, Postgres, Kubernetes, AWS. No legacy monoliths, no twenty-year-old framework. We use tools that make engineering enjoyable, not painful.

How our hiring actually works.

No ten-round interviews. No whiteboard puzzles. Just a conversation and a real piece of work.

STEP 01
Intro call

A 30-minute conversation with one of our founders. We learn about you, you learn about us. No pressure, no trick questions.

~30 minutes
STEP 02
Skills round

A technical or design conversation with the team you'd work with. For engineers, a short practical exercise — not a whiteboard test.

~1 hour
STEP 03
Paid trial

For most roles, a 1-week paid trial where you work on a real problem with us. Both sides find out if it's a fit before committing.

1 week
STEP 04
Offer

Within 3 days of the trial ending, we make a decision and send an offer if it's a yes. If it's a no, we tell you why — honestly.

Within 3 days
